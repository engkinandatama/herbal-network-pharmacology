# Compound References - Phaleria macrocarpa

## Literature Sources for Compounds

### Methods Statement for Paper

> "The bioactive compounds of *P. macrocarpa* were collected through literature review from phytochemical studies (Altaf et al., 2013; Hendra et al., 2011; Zhang et al., 2016; Kurnia et al., 2018; Susilawati et al., 2015; Easmin et al., 2015). A total of 26 compounds including flavonoids, benzophenones, lignans, and phenolic acids were identified. SMILES structures were obtained from PubChem database."
